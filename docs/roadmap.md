# Roadmap

This roadmap is intentionally staged around mastery, not raw problem count.

## Stage 0

Complexity and problem-solving fundamentals.

## Stage 1

Arrays, strings, and hashing.

## Stage 2

Linked lists, stack, queue, and deque.

## Stage 3

Two pointers, sliding window, prefix sum, and binary search.

## Stage 4

Sorting, recursion, and divide and conquer.

## Stage 5

Trees, binary search trees, heaps, and tries.

## Stage 6

Graphs, breadth-first search, depth-first search, and topological sort.

## Stage 7

Shortest paths, minimum spanning tree, and union-find.

## Stage 8

Greedy and backtracking.

## Stage 9

Dynamic programming.

## Stage 10

Advanced data structures.

## Stage 11

Advanced graph algorithms.

## Stage 12

Advanced mathematics and number theory.

## First Milestone

The current milestone only prepares the repository for:

- complexity
- arrays
- strings
- hashing
- two pointers
- sliding window

The next milestone is to add the first real Kotlin solutions and notes in those areas.
